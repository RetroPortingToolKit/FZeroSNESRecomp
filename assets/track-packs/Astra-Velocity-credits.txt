F-Zero Astra Velocity

Hack by MF9_05 / Worthy. Original F-Zero by Nintendo.
Uses Community Grand Prix components; see the bundled CGP credits for the
component authors.

Imported from Astra Velocity (CGP) P1, with twenty published courses in the
Eclipse, Oblivion, Vector and Apex cups. Source revision SHA-256:
795548cb23e48731ca5c34aed551165515e195f03db249e710fa0abe241a54f8

The resource patch is a course-data derivative, not the full ROM or a
replacement executable. The player supplies an original USA F-Zero ROM.
The port retains the authored names, ordering, SNES music selections and
CGP-compatible magnet mechanics. Illusion alone requires fall-down rules,
with the original gravity of this revision. Vehicle/global rule choices
remain independent. No Astra Velocity music recordings were supplied.

Course ZIPs are reconstructed editable FZEdit projects, not the author's
original working files. See docs/ASTRA_VELOCITY_IMPORT.md for scope and checks.
